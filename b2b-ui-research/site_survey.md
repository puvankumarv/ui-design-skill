**Sample:** 29 unique homepages (12 SaaS, 8 fintech, 9 developer tools) + 4 pricing pages + 10 pricing components. The archive leans toward modern startups; traditional enterprise and industrial B2B are under-represented (see gaps at the end).

| Pattern | Count | Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Centered stack, product visual below | 10 | 34% | Dominant in SaaS and dev tools |
| Type-only hero, no visual | 5 | 17% | Used by brands confident enough to skip a visual |
| Hero visual is **real product UI** (screenshot, mockup or live widget) | 17 | 59% | Highest-signal B2B pattern |

## Hero headline measurements (from fold breakdowns)

- Display-to-body size ratio: usually **3:1**, up to 6:1 for type-only heroes.
- Headline length: 2–3 lines on desktop, 4–5 when the hero is split.
- Hero fits inside 1280×800: headline, subhead, CTA and the top of the visual.
- Single accent colour, used **only** on CTAs, one highlighted headline word, and small UI marks. Never on body text.
- Two-tone headline (one phrase in accent colour) seen on Arrows, Superlist, Razorpay, Hex.

## Pricing pages (4 pages + 10 components)

- Monthly/annual toggle with a savings badge: Amie, Anytype, Beehiiv, Descript.
- 2–4 plan cards; the recommended plan is marked by a "Most popular" label, a coloured border or a filled CTA.
- Price unit always explicit: "per user / month", "billed annually".
- "Everything in X, plus:" list pattern on the higher tiers (Netlify, Amie).
- Usage selector before plans when price depends on volume (Beehiiv: subscriber count dropdown).
- Full feature comparison table below the cards (Netlify link, Linear table).
- Enterprise tier: "Contact sales", no price.

## Gaps in the archive

1. **Traditional enterprise and industrial B2B** (manufacturing, logistics, wholesale portals, B2B ecommerce): very few rows. Covered partly by the live pass (Flexport, Salesforce) and should get a dedicated study before release.
2. **App UI** (dashboards, tables, settings, onboarding): the archive stores marketing pages only. App UI rules come from public design systems instead (`design_system_rules.md`).
3. Archive ranking was lexical-only during this pass, so the result sets are good but not exhaustive.
