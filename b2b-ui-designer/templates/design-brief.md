# Design brief

Ask at most 5 questions per turn, the most decisive first. Fill the rest with the defaults in `SKILL.md` and state them.

## Intake questions (in priority order)

1. **What are we designing?** Page type (homepage, pricing, feature, solution, security, demo, app screen) or an audit of an existing URL.
2. **How do customers buy?** Self-serve with a card, a sales conversation, both, or a quote. Are prices public?
3. **Who decides and who uses it?** The buyer's role and company size; the day-to-day user if different.
4. **What proof is real and available?** Customer logos you may show, named quotes, measurable results, ratings, certifications, analyst reports.
5. **Brand and references:** logo, colours, fonts, sites you like (and what you like about them), competitors.
6. Product visuals: are there screenshots or a demo video we can use?
7. Output format: single HTML file, React + Tailwind, or a spec for a designer?
8. Constraints: CMS or framework, languages, accessibility commitments (VPAT), regions.
