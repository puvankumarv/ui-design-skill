Questions finance teams ask first

How long does setup take?

[Client to confirm.] Connect your ledger, import last month's checklist, and assign owners. Most of the work is the import.

Does it replace our accounting system?

No. Closeline sits next to your ledger and reads from it. Journal entries are still posted in your accounting system.

Who can see what?

Permissions are set per role and per entity. Reviewers and auditors can be given read-only access.

What does it cost?

Free for up to 3 users. Paid plans are priced per user per month; see pricing.
