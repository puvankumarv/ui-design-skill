Billing questions

Who counts as a user?

Anyone who prepares or approves close tasks. Read-only reviewers and auditors are free.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. Switching to annual applies the lower price from your next billing date; unused monthly time is credited.

What happens when a trial ends?

You choose a plan or drop to Free. Nothing is deleted; features above Free become read-only.

Do you send invoices and accept purchase orders?

Business and Enterprise can pay by invoice. Enterprise contracts can include purchase orders and security questionnaires.
