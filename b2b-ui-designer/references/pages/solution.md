# Solution pages (by industry or by role)

**Master decision:** whose pains lead the page. Name the audience in the headline and keep every section about them.

## Section order

1. `hero`: "[Product] for [audience]" + an outcome that audience cares about + a UI view configured for them
2. `pains`: 3 problems in the audience's own words
3. `features`: how the product solves each pain, one block per pain, in the same order
4. `proof`: a case study from the same industry or role. The logo strip is filtered to that industry
5. `features` (grid): other relevant capabilities
6. `security`: regulations that matter to this industry (HIPAA for health, PCI DSS for payments, SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001)
7. `cta-band`: often "Talk to a [industry] specialist"

## Rules

- Industry pages speak the industry's language (claims, shipments, policies, SKUs); role pages speak the role's KPIs (close time for finance, pipeline for sales, uptime for engineering).
- Reuse the main product's components and tokens. Only content and screenshots change.
- Don't promise compliance you can't prove. Each badge links to evidence on the trust page.
- Several people take part in a B2B purchase. Link sideways to role pages ("For IT: security and SSO →"). `[NNG]`
