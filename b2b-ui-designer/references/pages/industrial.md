# Industrial, logistics, services and B2B ecommerce

**Master decision:** catalogue-first (buyers know what they want) or quote-first (buyers need help specifying).

> **Thin evidence.** The reference archive barely covers this segment. The live pass included Flexport (logistics) and Salesforce. Treat these rules as a starting point, say so to the user, and study 3–5 sites from the client's own industry before designing.

## Homepage order (quote-first: logistics, manufacturing services, agencies)

1. `hero`: what you do + for whom + where (regions, capacity), real photography of operations, "Request a quote" + "Talk to an expert"
2. `logos`: customers or certifications (ISO 9001, industry bodies)
3. `services`: one card per service line (Flexport: ocean, air, customs, fulfilment, control tower)
4. `proof`: numbers that matter in the industry (on-time %, capacity, years, sites), each with a source
5. `how-it-works`: from enquiry to delivery in 3–5 steps
6. `industries`: sectors served
7. `insights`: market updates, rate reports, regulations (Flexport puts these near the end)
8. `newsletter` or `cta-band`
9. `footer`: locations, phone, certifications

## Catalogue-first (distributors, B2B ecommerce, parts suppliers)

- Search is the main navigation: a large search box with part-number support, and the category tree in a mega menu.
- Product list: filters for specs (size, material, standard), dense table view **and** grid view, price per unit and price breaks, stock and lead time.
- Product page: spec table, downloadable datasheets and CAD files, compliance documents, quantity-break pricing, "Add to quote" next to "Add to cart".
- Account features up front: reorder, saved lists, quotes, invoices, multiple buyers per company, approval workflows.

## Rules

- Phone and email visible in the header; many buyers still call.
- Show real facilities, fleets and people. Industrial buyers distrust stock imagery.
- Specs in tables with units, never in prose paragraphs.
- Style direction: usually `enterprise`. Dense, clear, low motion.
